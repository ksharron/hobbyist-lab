Music & Audio

Make a little noise. You don’t need an instrument to start.

Singing

Pick a song you enjoy and start with one verse. Listen once, then sing along at a comfortable pitch and volume.

Spend 15 minutes getting familiar with the melody. Humming counts, too.

Body percussion

Turn claps, finger snaps, and gentle taps into a rhythm. Start with a simple pattern and repeat it steadily.

Try four beats: clap, tap, tap, clap. Once it feels comfortable, change one beat or play along with a favorite song.
