Outdoor & Adventure

Find something new to notice, close to home.

Birdwatching

Start at a window, in your backyard, or at a nearby park. Spend 15 minutes watching and listening.

Pick one bird and notice its size, colors, movement, and sounds. You don’t need binoculars to begin.

Nature walks

Choose a familiar local route and give yourself time to explore at your own pace.

For your first walk, look for three different leaf shapes, listen for two distinct sounds, and notice one thing you usually pass by.
