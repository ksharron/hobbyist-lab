Creative & Crafting

Start with what you have. Make something just because you can.

Drawing

All you need for your first session is a pencil and paper. Pick an everyday object and spend 15 minutes sketching it.

Try noticing its outline, the spaces around it, and where the shadows fall. Your drawing doesn’t need to be perfect.

Collage

Gather scrap paper, an old magazine, or packaging you like. Tear or cut out shapes, arrange them, and glue them onto paper.

For a first project, choose one color and build a small composition around it.
